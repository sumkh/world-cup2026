New to football? How to read this table ▾

Each group has 4 teams. Every team plays the other three once. Teams earn points for each match: 3 for a win, 1 for a draw (tie), 0 for a loss. The table is sorted by points — the top two (highlighted) go through to the knockout rounds.

PPlayed — matches played so far

GFGoals For — goals the team scored
GAGoals Against — goals it let in
GDGoal Difference — GF minus GA (e.g. +3); breaks ties when teams are level on points
PtsPoints — the ranking number (3·wins + 1·draws)

If teams are level on Pts, the higher GD ranks first, then most goals scored (GF).
